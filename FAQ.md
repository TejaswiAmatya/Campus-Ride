## Lab 1

### Can not merge unrelated histories

You might have initialized and pushed some random files to you repo before pulling the data from the assignments repo. To solve this try pushing to your own repository with the --allow-unrelated-histories command. You can read more about unrelated histories here: [https://www.educative.io/answers/the-fatal-refusing-to-merge-unrelated-histories-git-error](https://www.educative.io/answers/the-fatal-refusing-to-merge-unrelated-histories-git-error)
